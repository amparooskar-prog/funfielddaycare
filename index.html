A FEW THINGS PARENTS ASK

Have another question? Email us
Which program is right for my child?

Tell us your child’s age, the kind of support you’re looking for, and your preferred schedule. Our team can explain the child care, playschool, Kinder Prep, and tutorial options.

Can we visit before enrolling?

Yes, message us to arrange a daycare tour. Please confirm your visit with the team before coming.

Do you offer drop-in child care?

Fun Field has announced drop-in daily child care. Contact us to confirm availability, requirements, and the schedule for your preferred date.

Where can I get fees and schedules?

Message our Facebook page, call 0991 947 1535, or email funfielddaycare@gmail.com for current fees, schedules, and enrollment information.
